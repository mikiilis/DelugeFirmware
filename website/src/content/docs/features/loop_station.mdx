The Loop Station is an eight-track, RAM-resident performance looper. It records and overdubs audio into independent tracks while keeping live playback separate from SD-card writes. The feature is disabled by default.

## Enable and enter

1. Open **Settings → Community Features** and turn on **Enable Loop Station**.
2. Return to the idle Song/Session View root. Press **CROSS-SCREEN** once, without holding Shift, to open Loop Station.

The mode reserves its configured audio memory before it opens. If memory cannot be reserved, it stays in the current view and reports the failure. Starting the view does not start transport or recording. CROSS-SCREEN or SONG exits to Song/Session View; an in-progress capture can make exit wait until it is safe.

## Track layout

The 16×8 pad grid contains eight 4×4 track tiles. Tracks 1–4 are across the top and 5–8 across the bottom. The middle 2×2 pads in each tile control that track. The twelve surrounding pads form its selection and progress ring.

- Press a tile’s centre to record an empty track. Press it again to finish the first take; the **Record Action** setting chooses whether the new phrase plays or immediately enters overdub.
- Press the centre of a playing track to begin overdubbing. Press again to close the capture gate. A quick double press on a playing track stops it.
- Select a track from its border ring. The ring fills clockwise from the upper-left pad as the phrase plays and resets on each wrap.
- Stop a track, then hold its centre for two seconds and release to clear it. The red sweep shows the hold progress.
- Use **PLAY** for Start All and **RECORD** for Record on the selected track. The physical top row of the sidebar has Start All (left) and Stop All (right); other sidebar rows are inactive.
- Press **BACK** on a track with available history to request undo. History restoration can take time; the display reports when undo is unavailable or pending.

Track colours identify tracks. New tracks default to **Yellow, Cyan, Blue, Purple, Magenta, Pink, Gold and Lime** for tracks 1–8 respectively. The centre and ring also distinguish empty, recording, overdubbing, playing and stopped states; these temporary state colours take priority over the chosen identity colour. A capture error is shown on its track and acknowledged by pressing its centre.

## Settings and ordinary clip access

Press **SELECT** for global settings. Hold **SHIFT** and press a track’s centre to open its settings. Turn the Select encoder to move through rows, press Select to edit, and press **BACK** to return.

Global settings include Record Action, Start Mode/Interval and Stop Mode/Interval. Interval rows appear only when the corresponding mode is Synced. Track settings include input source, monitoring, record duration, Loop or One Shot playback, transpose, colour and Copy To Track. Transpose is adjustable in cents within ±24.50 semitones. Copy To Track copies settings only; the destination keeps its audio, name, output, effects and history. A busy or unavailable destination is left unchanged.

Change a ring's identity colour with **SHIFT + track centre → Colour → SELECT to edit → turn SELECT → SELECT to finish → BACK**. The ring updates live while the menu shows the chosen colour. White remains selectable, and tracks may share colours. Choices persist through normal Song **Save/load**, without an SD write on each encoder turn. Existing saved/custom colours, including White and copied or duplicate choices, are preserved; new defaults do not recolour saved Songs.

Press **CLIP VIEW** to open the selected track’s ordinary Audio Clip view and menus. Loop tracks use normal song outputs and effects. Loop Station does not replace the normal clip, Song or Arranger workflows.

## Memory, Save and history

Active phrases are stored in a shared audio-memory pool. The current configured profile reserves 32 MiB for active stereo audio, shared by all eight tracks. At 44.1 kHz and eight bytes per frame, that is about 95 seconds of combined audio before page overhead, or about 11.9 seconds per track if divided evenly. Tracks can have different lengths, and their actual duration limit depends on pages currently available. This arithmetic is not a measured hardware guarantee; the device memory and audio-load acceptance matrix remains unrun.

Capturing and overdubbing do not wait for SD writes. Use the normal Song **SAVE** workflow to make a durable checkpoint. The save can remain pending while a consistent snapshot is written. Audio edited after the save snapshot remains unsaved until a later successful save. A card error leaves the current RAM audio available but does not make that audio durable.

Undo history is bounded. Under memory or storage pressure, older history may be discarded while current capture continues. The view’s status indicates unsaved audio and whether undo is available. Loading a Song restores its committed loop checkpoints; incomplete or failed saves do not replace the last committed manifest. Saved audio uses cumulative 24-bit, 44.1 kHz WAV checkpoints, so reload is not a bit-identical 32-bit RAM round trip.

## Validation status

Host tests cover loop timing, both Record Actions, source/monitor routing, long and short phrase behavior, rapid gestures, history pressure, archive faults and restart recovery. These tests do not measure available hardware memory, full audio callback deadlines, physical input latency or recorded output quality. OLED and seven-segment device inspection, hardware memory/DSP measurements, and physical audio-capture acceptance remain **NOT_RUN**.
